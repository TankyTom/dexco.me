Common questions

How do I swap the Dock’s Downloads stack for Landed?

Right-click the Downloads stack at the right end of your Dock and choose Remove from Dock. The first time Landed opens it shows a small card with a Downloads tile: drag that tile into the Dock, between the divider and the Bin. Click it once and you’re set. Your Downloads folder itself isn’t touched.

I removed Landed’s tile from the Dock. How do I get it back?

Open Landed from Applications or Spotlight and the card appears again. Or open Settings, find Show Landed in, and click Add to Dock… under The Dock, next to the Bin.

Can I use Landed from the menu bar instead?

Yes. In Landed’s Settings, under Show Landed in, tick The menu bar. Tick any mix of the menu bar and the two Dock spots; Landed won’t let you untick the last one. Settings is always the gear at the top right of the shelf.

The shortcut doesn’t open Landed, or it moves my windows.

Another app is probably using ⌃⌥D, often a window manager. Open Landed’s Settings, click the shortcut and press a new one.

Why don’t some files say where they came from?

Landed shows what macOS and the app that saved the file recorded. Browsers and Mail record it; files made on your Mac, or copied in from another folder, often have nothing to show. In Free, the source shows on the newest file; Pro shows it on every file and lets you search by site.

Which browsers show live progress?

Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox all show a live bar with the percentage and size. Firefox doesn’t share an estimate of time left, so its rows leave that out. Chrome and Edge don’t share a download’s name until it finishes, so those rows can say “Unnamed download” until then.

Can Landed cancel a download?

Yes. Click the × on a download in progress and Landed cancels it in Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and clears away the partial file. If a browser ever doesn’t respond, Landed opens that browser’s own Downloads list instead.

Will Landed ever delete my files on its own?

Not unless you turn on a cleanup rule, and they’re all off to start with. Rules only move files to the Archive folder or the Bin, never delete them outright, never touch pinned files, and always show an Undo.

How do I restore Landed Pro on another Mac?

Pro unlocks on any Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. If it doesn’t, open Landed’s Settings and choose Restore Purchases.

How do I uninstall Landed?

If you’ve stashed any files, bring them back first from the bar at the top of the shelf. Then drag Landed’s tile out of the Dock, quit Landed, and drag it from Applications to the Bin. To remove its settings and cache too, delete ~/Library/Containers/me.dexco.Landed.

Does Landed slow down my Mac or drain the battery?

No. It uses well under 1% of one CPU core while idle and only does real work when your Downloads folder changes or the shelf is open.
